## Authority and law

- Authority: Ministerio de Salud, Dirección Nacional de Farmacia y Drogas (DNFD). DE 27. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Law 419 of 1 Feb 2024 subrogates Law 1 of 10 Jan 2001. Q&A. Checked 2026-11-24.
- DE 27 regulates Law 419 (Art. 1). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Registration must meet Law 419 Art. 29 and the norms adopted by DNFD in the RTCA or other international norms (DE 27 Art. 20). Checked 2026-11-24.

## Procedure

- Digital file on the DNFD platform (Art. 16). Filed by a lawyer with refrendo of the responsible pharmacist and of the Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos; poder to the lawyer from the holder, manufacturer or legal representative (Art. 19). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Abbreviated procedure for medicines registered and marketed in countries with high standard authorities recognized by WHO/PAHO and set by executive decree (Art. 18); no prior analytical tests (Art. 21); quality control of the first imported lot within six months (Art. 256). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Observations: three months to correct, otherwise returned and must be refiled (Art. 22). Checked 2026-11-24.
- A legal representative residing in Panama, authorized by the holder, is required to register from abroad. Q&A. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Medicines made in Central America register through mutual recognition. Q&A. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Change of holder: application signed by the profesional responsable, legal document supporting the change, packaging, payment (DE 27, modification requirements, "Cambio de titular"). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Holders, national and foreign labs and distributors must be able to report suspected adverse reactions with MedDRA and WHODrug (Art. 318). Labs, holders or distributors designate a local pharmacist as Responsable de los Controles Post-Registro (Art. 257). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Profesional responsable definition mirrors the RTCA (Art. 2, item 131). Checked 2026-11-24.
