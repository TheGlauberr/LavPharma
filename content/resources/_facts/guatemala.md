## Authority

- MSPAS regulates registro sanitario de referencia, manufacture, import, sale (AG 712-99 Art. 2). Department = Departamento de Regulación y Control de Productos Farmacéuticos y Afines (DRCPFA), under the Dirección General de Regulación, Vigilancia y Control de la Salud (DGRVCS); LNS = Laboratorio Nacional de Salud. Source: F-AS-p-14 flowchart https://medicamentos.mspas.gob.gt/phocadownload/Guias_y_Procedimientos/PROCESO%20DE%20INGRESO%20REGISTRO%20SANITARIO%2022032022.pdf. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Products needing registro sanitario de referencia: especialidades farmacéuticas, biológicos, radiofármacos, homeopáticos (AG 712-99 Art. 10). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Código de Salud Art. 167 (quoted in AM 263-2025): registro = specifications that serve as pattern; 5 years; under responsibility of a university professional of the field. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Draft new regulation to replace AG 712-99, watermarked "BORRADOR EN REVISIÓN", header "ACUERDO GUBERNATIVO XX-2025": https://medicamentos.mspas.gob.gt/phocadownload//legislacion-vigente/2026/REFORMAS-AG-712-99.pdf. NOT in force. Checked 2026-11-25.

## Registration

- Application signed by químico farmacéutico responsable (AG 712-99 Art. 11; definition 7.38). Pharmacist responsible for formula and purity, shared with company legal rep (Art. 30). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Art. 13.1: representation document from titular (13.1.1); CPP valid 2 years (13.1.10); for a new product, consent of the originator or evidence approval took effect more than 5 years ago (13.1.12); standards and samples for LNS (13.1.13, 13.1.14); GMP certificate (13.1.16); patent sworn statement, not processed without it (13.1.18). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Denial if manufacturer or distributor lacks licencia sanitaria (Art. 24.7). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Renewal every 5 years at holder's request; if not filed before expiry, cancelled (Art. 25). Checked 2026-11-25.
- LNS tests in the procedure (Art. 23); flowchart F-AS-p-14: pay 63-A2 receipts DRCPFA and LNS, file at DRCPFA and LNS, LNS issues certificate of analysis, DRCPFA issues registration after LNS passes. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Summary table (Dec 2022): especialidad farmacéutica pays DRCPFA + LNS and delivers dossier to LNS; homologados and new-molecule biologics: samples and standards at first import; mutual recognition and renewals: no LNS file, post-market sampling. https://medicamentos.mspas.gob.gt/phocadownload/Aranceles/RESUMEN%20PAGO%20DE%20ARANCELES%2015122022.pdf. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Forms: F-AS-f-04 registration, F-AS-f-05 renewal, F-AS-f-09 update, F-AS-d-01 composition, F-AS-p-01 mutual recognition. Page also says 5 years validity. https://medicamentos.mspas.gob.gt/index.php/servicios/autorizacion-de-productos-farmaceuticos-y-afines/registros-sanitarios. Checked 2026-11-25.
- SIAMED Web video tutorials include "Solicitud de Registro Sanitario"; platform https://siamedweb.mspas.gob.gt/user/. https://medicamentos.mspas.gob.gt/index.php/siamed-web/videotutoriales. Checked 2026-11-25. Launch date (3 Nov 2025) only in press (Emisoras Unidas), not official.
- AM 263-2025 (17 Oct 2025): paper files processed alongside electronic files in filing order (sec. 6); files not corrected in 3 months archived (sec. 7); reliance for products registered in RTCA states or by WHO high-maturity authorities (4.1); NT 77 files get simplified review of CPP, labelling, stability (5.1); renewals without changes on sworn statement (5.3); quotes Decreto 5-2021 Art. 28 (archive files not moved for more than three months). Checked 2026-11-25.

## Renewal

- NT 90 (Apr 2023): renewal may be filed up to the last day of validity (Art. 2). Checked 2026-11-25.
- NT 27-2010: after expiry = new registration (5.2); requirements incl. sworn no-change statement, CPP/CLV, GMP (Art. 6). Its Art. 2 (file one year before, granted within 45 days) appears superseded by NT 90 for the filing window; 45 days not used in articles. Checked 2026-11-25.

## Homologation (NT 77 v8-2023)

- Scope: chemical synthesis specialties and vaccines registered by WHO high maturity authorities on DRCPFA's official lists (Art. 1, 2). Checked 2026-11-25.
- 5 years validity (6.1); one registration per country or manufacturing lab (6.2); distributors need licencia sanitaria (6.3); Spanish or sworn translation, foreign docs per Ley del Organismo Judicial Arts. 37 and 38 (6.6). Checked 2026-11-25.
- CPP from listed authority and within its WHO scope (7.5); extra CPP/CLV from a high-maturity authority where marketed if not marketed in issuing country (6.14.4); first lot notification (F-AS-f-19) for LNS check (Art. 9); post-market sampling (Art. 10). Checked 2026-11-25.
