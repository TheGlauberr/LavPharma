## RTCA 65.05.51:18

- Approved by Res. 436-2020 (COMIECO-XCIII), 10 Dec 2020; replaced RTCA 65.05.51:08 (Res. 257-2010) by total substitution; repealed Res. 257-2010, 326-2013, 360-2014, 362-2015; in force 10 Aug 2021. Source: Res. 436-2020. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Panama MIDA confirms in force 10 Aug 2021 (Comunicado DR-01-2025, 9 Apr 2025): https://mida.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Comunicado-DR-01-2025-Estudio-de-residuos.pdf. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Res. 436-2020: 5 years from entry into force to implement CAMEVET (WOAH) GMP code and guide; meanwhile a member state manufacturer registering in another state files an official document backing its GMP operating plan. Annex H coding: 24 months (Panama 30). Checked 2026-11-25. A later COMIECO resolution (478-2023) reportedly extended the coding deadline; only seen on leybook.com, not verified officially, not used.
- Committee: GT MAGA-VISAR, SV MAG, NI IPSA, HN SAG (SENASA), CR MAG-SENASA, PA MIDA (Informe). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Scope excludes raw materials and veterinary magistral preparations (2). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Definitions: oficina registrante (3.33), registrante can be the holder (3.41), representante legal (3.43), regente veterinario (3.44), titular (3.45). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Establishments must be registered, incl. oficinas registrantes and titulares no fabricantes (4); legal rep in the country, poder (not for establishments in GT, HN, PA, Nota 1), regente (4.1); 5 years, renewal from 3 months before (4.4). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Risk groups I to IV (5.2); registro común and simplificado (5.3, 5.4; list in Annex J); Annex K not subject to registration. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Registro común requirements (5.3.1): form A1/A2 signed by regente and registrante; notarized poder; original CLV (Annex B); formula; methods; CoA commercial lot (pilot allowed except biologics); labels; studies per Annex C with Spanish summary; sample and standard if requested; maquila contract (Annex D); payment. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Renewal (5.5): form A5, notarized manufacturer no-change document, CLV, poder. Filing window 3 months before to expiry; marketing allowed during renewal; after expiry new registration; 3 months to keep number, no marketing (6 h). Checked 2026-11-25.
- General (6): legal and consular formalities (a); CLV max 2 years, CoA 2 years (b); single trade name (e); one registration per manufacturer (s). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Validity 5 years from inscription (9); imports only with registration or in renewal (10); modifications only per Annex E, else new registration (12); cancellation grounds (13); labels in Spanish (14). Checked 2026-11-25.
- Annex B CLV: "a solicitud del gobierno de (nombre del país)"; complementary document for non-CA CLVs; export certificate if not marketed in origin. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Annex C: generics need bioequivalence (A.5); residue/withdrawal studies when applicable (A.4); Appendix I calendar by active group, separate columns for CR and for GT, SV, HN, NI, PA; per product. Checked 2026-11-25.
- Annex M recognition: only products originating in the member states (1); applicant registered with the recognizing authority (8.2); validity follows original (5.7, 7); changes made in origin country (4.2). Checked 2026-11-25.
