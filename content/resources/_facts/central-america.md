## RTCA 11.03.59:18

- Approved by COMIECO Res. 446-2021 on 28 Apr 2021; in force 3 Jan 2022; replaced RTCA 11.03.59:11 and the MR procedure of Res. 333-2013, and repealed Res. 333-2013 (Resuelve 1 to 4). Signed for CR, SV, GT, HN, NI, PA. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Committee: GT MSPAS, SV DNM, HN ARSA, CR Ministerio de Salud, NI Ministerio de Salud, PA Ministerio de Salud (Informe). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Procedure for registration, renewal and changes follows each country's law (6.18). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Validity 5 years, renewable for similar periods (6.2, 10). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Certificates valid at filing; 2 years if no validity stated (6.3). Originals or legalized copies, Spanish or translation (6.4). Foreign documents legalized under national rules (6.6). Checked 2026-11-24.
- One registration per country or lab; not in SV (fabricación alterna) (6.14 Nota 1). Checked 2026-11-24.
- IP and therapeutic equivalence national (6.16, 6.17); sanctions national (6.19). RTCA replaces national registration requirements except IP and bioequivalence (15). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Scope notes: magistral preparations excluded; supplements, homeopathics, radiopharmaceuticals, biologics and biotech, orphan drugs under national law until a regional rule exists (2, Notas). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Strict authority CPP accepted in CR, GT, SV, HN for first registration of products not documented in official literature (7.3 Nota 1). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Panama: long term stability zone IVb only (7.10 Nota 1). Product unit only NI and PA (7.14 Nota 1). Standards and samples after registration (7.12/7.13 Nota 1). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Renewal any time before expiry keeps the number; after expiry a new registration; 6 months to keep the number with justified cause, no marketing meanwhile; renewal not granted until requested changes are approved (9). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Definitions: 4.21 fabricación por terceros (holder responsible for the product); 4.23 laboratorio fabricante; 4.29 país de origen (where manufactured); 4.31 país del titular; 4.37 profesional responsable; 4.39 representante legal (resides in the country); 4.41 titular (owner of the product). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Annex 1: change of holder (A.11) needs prior approval: payment, application by the profesional responsable, new labels, document from the holder or its legal representative declaring the change, manufacturing contract with the new holder (per 7.4). Change of legal representative or profesional responsable (A.17): payment, application, poder. Change or addition of distributor (B.5): notification only, with a legal document from the holder or its legal representative. Changes can be requested by the profesional responsable if the poder allows it (Annex 1 intro). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Annex 2 (application content): holder data, distributor data (optional for El Salvador, item 4 Nota 1), legal representative, profesional responsable. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Cancellation for false sworn renewal statement (12.7) and at the holder's request (12.8). Renewal sworn statement by holder, legal rep, or profesional responsable with poder (9.1.3). Checked 2026-11-24.

## Mutual recognition (Annex II of Res. 446-2021)

- Scope: "todo registro sanitario de medicamentos originarios de los Estados Parte". Checked 2026-11-24.
- Decision in 10 business days, Panama 30; RM code plus country initial; validity equals the original registration (2 c, d). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Power of attorney to a person residing permanently in the recognizing country (1 c). Panama: Colegio Nacional de Farmacéuticos refrendo (1 b Nota 1); dossier copy at the start (1 e). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Bioequivalence and data protection national (8 d, e); no marketing without notifying changes (8 c); SV alternate manufacturers need separate recognition per manufacturer (8 g). Checked 2026-11-24.
- Panama DNFD reads it as the route for medicines manufactured in Central America. Source: https://minsa.gob.pa/sites/default/files/general/convertario_24_de_mayo_de_2024.pdf. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Res. 333-2013 (repealed) said it would not enter into force for Panama until a further COMIECO act. Source: https://srs.gob.sv/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/RESOLUCION-333-2013.pdf. Checked 2026-11-24.

## PAHO joint evaluation mechanism

- Receives dossiers from 1 Aug 2020; NRAs of CR, SV, GT, HN, NI, PA; chemical synthesis medicines not required to show bioequivalence; no central fee, local fees apply. Source: https://www.paho.org/en/news/28-7-2020-central-american-mechanism-joint-evaluation-medicines-begins-receive-dossiers. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Single technical review, under three months; the 2021 note says it was launched in Oct 2019 by CR, SV, GT, HN. Source: https://www.paho.org/en/news/25-6-2021-paho-presents-central-american-mechanism-joint-evaluation-medicines-pharmaceutical. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Ends in a single technical review attached to each country's registration process; June 2022 directors meeting reviewed the status. Source: https://www.paho.org/en/news/10-6-2022-directors-regulatory-authorities-central-america-oversight-effective-solutions. Checked 2026-11-24.
- Guatemala MSPAS joined in Aug 2022; DRCPFA evaluates; each product enters each country with its own registration. Source: https://agn.gt/salud-se-une-a-mecanismo-de-evaluacion-de-medicamentos-en-centroamerica/. Checked 2026-11-24.
