Researched on 2026-11-25. Source numbers match the `sources` list in en.md and es.md (same order).

Slug and title check: the backlog named "RTCA 65.05.51", which is correct. The current version is RTCA 65.05.51:18 (first revision), approved by COMIECO Resolution 436-2020, so the title says ":18" and the slug is unchanged.

Note on sources: source 1 is a text PDF of the RTCA annex hosted by Guatemala's MAGA. Source 2 is the signed resolution in FAO's FAOLEX database; no copy on a government site was found, and source 3 (Panama MIDA) independently confirms the 10 August 2021 entry into force. Res. 478-2023, which reportedly extended the Annex H coding deadline, was only found on a private site and is not used.
