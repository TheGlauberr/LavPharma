## What RTCA 65.05.51:18 is

It is the Central American technical regulation that sets the registration and control rules for veterinary medicines, related products, and the establishments that register, make, sell, split or store them.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> Its full Spanish name is *Medicamentos Veterinarios, Productos Afines y sus Establecimientos. Requisitos de Registro Sanitario y Control*.

COMIECO Resolution 436-2020, of 10 December 2020, fully replaced the previous version, RTCA 65.05.51:08 of 2010, and repealed the resolutions that had amended it. The new version entered into force on **10 August 2021**.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup> Panama's authority confirms that date in a 2025 notice.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

The registration of raw materials and of veterinary compounded (magistral) preparations is outside the regulation and follows each country's law.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Who registers in each country

The RTCA always refers to "the competent authority" of each member state without naming it. The committee that drafted the regulation included:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

| Country | Institution on the committee |
|---|---|
| Guatemala | Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Food (MAGA-VISAR) |
| El Salvador | Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock (MAG) |
| Honduras | Secretariat of Agriculture and Livestock (SENASA) |
| Nicaragua | Institute of Agricultural Protection and Health (IPSA) |
| Costa Rica | Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock (MAG-SENASA) |
| Panama | Ministry of Agricultural Development (MIDA) |

Watch El Salvador. The law of the Superintendencia de Regulación Sanitaria (SRS), in force since 2024, gives the SRS the power to grant registrations for pharmaceutical products for both human and veterinary use.<sup><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup> Check with us which institution takes your type of product today.

## There is no single regional registration

As with human medicines, the RTCA harmonizes requirements, but each country grants its own registration. On approval, the authority enters the product in its register, assigns a number under the harmonized coding in Annex H, and issues the registration certificate.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> Commercial imports are only authorized for products with a current registration (or one being renewed).<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Establishments are registered too

The RTCA requires every veterinary establishment to be registered. The list includes manufacturers, splitters, traders (importers, exporters, wholesalers and distributors), pharmacies, warehouses, **registration offices** (oficinas registrantes) and **non-manufacturing registration holders**.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

The general requirements for an establishment are:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- An operating authorization under each country's rules.
- A **legal representative in the country** of registration.
- A power of attorney granted by the registration holder. Guatemala, Honduras and Panama do not ask for this power to register the establishment, but they do ask for it to register products.
- A **veterinary technical director** (regente veterinario).
- Incorporation documents for a company, or identity documents for an individual.

An establishment registration lasts five years, and its renewal is filed from three months before expiry.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Who is who in a veterinary registration

The RTCA uses different terms from the human medicines regulation:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- **Holder or owner of the registration (titular o propietario):** the individual or company in whose favor a product's registration is granted, for marketing it.
- **Registrant (registrante):** the individual or company authorized by the holder to register the product with the authority. It can be the holder itself.
- **Legal representative:** whoever represents the holder and answers to the authority for the registration.
- **Veterinary technical director (regente veterinario):** the veterinarian authorized to run the technical side of veterinary establishments.

The application is signed and sealed by the regente and the registrant, with a notarized power of attorney from the holder in favor of the registrant.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> See [what a registration holder is](/en/resources/what-is-a-registration-holder) to decide whose name the registration should be in.

## Two types of registration

The RTCA sorts products into four risk groups, from restricted products sold only on a retained prescription to over-the-counter products, and defines two types of registration:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- **Standard registration (registro sanitario común)**, for all veterinary medicines and related products not on the simplified registration list.
- **Simplified registration (registro sanitario simplificado)**, for products on the harmonized list in Annex J. A simplified registration does not mean the product can be sold over the counter.

Annex K lists veterinary products that do not need registration.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Documents for a standard registration

Section 5.3.1 asks for:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- Application on form A1 or A2 of Annex A, signed and sealed by the regente and the registrant.
- Notarized power of attorney from the holder in favor of the registrant. If a current power already covers registering, renewing and changing the product, it does not need to be filed again.
- Original **Certificate of Free Sale (CFS)** issued by the authority of the country of origin, following Annex B.
- Complete qualitative and quantitative formula, as an original.
- Physical, chemical and biological test methods, internationally recognized or validated by the manufacturer.
- Certificate of analysis of a commercial batch (or a pilot batch if the product has not been marketed, except for biologics).
- Draft labels, insert and packaging.
- Scientific studies or recognized literature supporting efficacy, stability, safety and quality for each species, under Annex C, with a summary in Spanish.
- A sample and an analytical standard, when the authority asks for them.
- A contract manufacturing (maquila) agreement when a company other than the holder makes the product.
- Proof of payment.

Annex C sets out the technical support. For drugs it includes, among other items, the manufacturing process, stability studies and, where applicable, residue depletion or withdrawal period verification studies. Generics add bioequivalence studies, and new molecules add safety tests in the target species, clinical efficacy studies and studies of unwanted effects.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Fixed dose combinations must show an advantage of the combination over each ingredient on its own.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## The Certificate of Free Sale

Annex B sets the content of the CFS: it must be an original, current, with the corresponding consular formalities, and its text names the government of the country requesting it.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> So plan one CFS for each destination country. Other CFS rules:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- The countries recognize a maximum validity of two years from issue, or less if the certificate says so. It must be valid when the application is filed.
- If a CFS from a country outside Central America lacks any of the Annex B information, add a supplementary document from the same authority.
- If the product is not sold in its country of origin, file a certificate for export products that explains why it is not sold there.

All official documents from the country of origin and all powers of attorney must complete their legal and consular formalities.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> Labels, cartons and inserts are filed in Spanish.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Withdrawal periods: a calendar by molecule

For medicines used in food-producing species, Appendix I of Annex C requires each product's withdrawal period to be verified once in its commercial life. The studies are phased in by active ingredient group under a calendar, with different years for Costa Rica and for the other five countries.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> The verification is done per product, not per active ingredient.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

In 2025 Panama's authority reminded applicants that they were not filing this study and published its calendar: for example, enrofloxacin and florfenicol from 2023, and benzimidazoles (thiabendazole) and amphenicols (thiamphenicol) from 2026.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> Check which year applies to your molecule before you file a registration or a renewal.

## Good Manufacturing Practice

Resolution 436-2020 gave the countries five years from the RTCA's entry into force to implement the Code of Good Manufacturing Practice for veterinary medicines of the Committee of the Americas for Veterinary Medicines (CAMEVET) of the World Organisation for Animal Health. During that period, a manufacturer from a country in the region that registers in another country files an official document from its own authority backing its plan to meet GMP.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Recognition of registrations within the region

Annex M allows a registration already granted to be recognized in another country, but **only for products originating in the member states**.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> Its main points:<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- You file the application signed by the regente and the registrant, the power of attorney, copies of the application and certificate of analysis approved in the first country, the CFS with consular formalities, and the approved labels.
- The applicant must be registered with the authority of the recognizing country.
- The authority may ask for local efficacy and safety tests, with technical justification.
- The recognized registration keeps the validity of the original registration.
- Changes are made in the country of origin and then recognized in the others.

If your product is made outside Central America, you need a standard registration in each country.

## Validity, renewal and changes

- **Validity.** Five years from entry in the register, renewable for equal periods.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- **Renewal.** Filed from three months before up to the expiry date. While it is pending, the product can be imported and sold as long as the authority's requests are met. The file includes, among other items, form A5, a notarized document from the manufacturer stating there have been no legal, technical or scientific changes, and a current CFS.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- **If it expires.** You must file a new registration. Within three months after expiry you can ask, with justification, to keep the original number, but the product cannot be sold during that period.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- **Changes.** Only the changes listed in Annex E are handled as amendments to the registration; any other variation needs a separate registration.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- **Several manufacturers.** A product made by several manufacturers needs a registration for each manufacturer.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- **Name.** Each registration carries a single trade name.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## How to plan the work

1. **Confirm the product's category**: risk group, standard or simplified registration, or exempt.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
2. **Set up the team in each country**: holder, registrant, legal representative and veterinary technical director, and register the relevant establishment.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
3. **Request one CFS per destination country** and plan the consular formalities.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
4. **Check the withdrawal period calendar** if the product is used in food-producing animals.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a>, <a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
5. **Choose the route**: standard or simplified registration in each country, or recognition if the product is made in the region.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
