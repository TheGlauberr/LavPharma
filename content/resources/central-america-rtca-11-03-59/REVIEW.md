## Not verified, so not stated as fact

- The text of Panama's Law 419 itself. The Gaceta Oficial site blocks automated access with a captcha, so Law 419 claims rely on the DNFD Q&A and Executive Decree 27, both official MINSA documents.
- Current status of the joint evaluation mechanism after June 2022. ARSA's mechanism page cited by PAHO (https://arsa.gob.hn/paginas/mecanismoEvaluacion) returns 404.
- PAHO's 2021 note says the mechanism was launched in October 2019 by four authorities (Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras); its 2020 note lists six. The article reports the 2020 list as stated.
- Whether any country recognizes a registration of a product manufactured outside the region. The text and the DNFD answer point to no; not stated as an absolute rule.
- National fees and review times for Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Costa Rica and Panama (not researched for this explainer; left out).
- Which countries are currently on Panama's list of high standard authorities (set by a separate executive decree, not opened).
