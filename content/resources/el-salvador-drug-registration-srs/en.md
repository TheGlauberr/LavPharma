## Who regulates medicines in El Salvador

The Superintendencia de Regulación Sanitaria (SRS) is an autonomous public institution created by Legislative Decree 891 of 14 November 2023. Its law gives it the power to grant sanitary registrations, recognitions of registration, marketing authorizations, renewals and modifications for pharmaceutical products, among other regulated goods.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

The same law declared the Dirección Nacional de Medicamentos (DNM) in dissolution and states that the SRS succeeds it in all its functions. Any reference to the DNM in other laws or documents is now read as a reference to the SRS. The law entered into force on **7 August 2024**.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

The SRS applies the Ley de Medicamentos and the other laws already in force, and the regulations issued for the DNM stay valid as long as they do not contradict the new law.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> That is why the national rules below still carry the DNM name.

## The rules that apply

1. **RTCA 11.03.59:18**, the Central American technical regulation on registration requirements for human medicines. It was approved by COMIECO Resolution 446-2021 on 28 April 2021 and entered into force on **3 January 2022**.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>
2. **Ley de Medicamentos**, which sets the five year validity and the grounds to deny or cancel a registration.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
3. **Reglamento General de la Ley de Medicamentos**, whose Article 20 lists the national documents for a pharmaceutical product and says each product must also meet the RTCA.<sup><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

The RTCA leaves the administrative procedure for registration, renewal and modifications to each country's own legislation.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Who can hold the registration

The RTCA defines three roles:<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- **Holder (titular del registro):** the natural or legal person that owns the product.
- **Profesional responsable:** a pharmacist or chemist-pharmacist responsible for the registration file before the authority, authorized by the holder or its legal representative through a power of attorney.
- **Legal representative:** a natural or legal person that resides in the country where the registration is filed, authorized by the holder to answer to the authority.

The national regulation requires every profesional or apoderado responsable to be registered with the authority, with their power of attorney, before starting any procedure. It also says the holder must always have a profesional and an apoderado responsable in place.<sup><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Step by step

1. **Appoint your local team.** Grant the powers of attorney to the profesional responsable and, where used, the legal representative, and have them registered with the SRS.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a>, <a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
2. **Collect the manufacturer's documents** listed below, legalized and in Spanish.
3. **Check the product name.** The RTCA does not allow a brand name already used for a different active ingredient, or one used before for a product with a different indication.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>
4. **File the application.** The SRS says a registration for a pharmaceutical product can be filed online from a phone or computer, without visiting its offices.<sup><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup> Its renewal notice points to the electronic window at ventanilla.srs.gob.sv.<sup><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>
5. **Pay the fee** set in the SRS tarifario and attach the receipt.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a>, <a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>
6. **Answer observations.** The applicant has a maximum of one calendar year to correct the observations; otherwise the file is treated as abandoned and archived.<sup><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
7. **Receive the registration**, valid for five years.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Documents from the foreign manufacturer

Section 7 of RTCA 11.03.59:18 lists what a registration file must contain:<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- Application signed and sealed by the profesional responsable.
- Powers of attorney showing the legal and technical representation granted by the holder.
- WHO-type Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CPP). If that certificate is not issued, a Certificate of Free Sale plus a GMP certificate for each site involved in manufacturing, issued by the authority of the manufacturing country.
- Manufacturing contract, or the relevant extract, when the product is made by a third party.
- Complete quantitative and qualitative formula per dose unit.
- Product monograph based on the official literature.
- Validated analytical methods with the validation report.
- Finished product specifications.
- Labels for the primary and secondary packaging and the insert, or their drafts.
- Stability study report. The national regulation asks for a study for climatic zone IV.<sup><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- Safety and efficacy studies when the product's safety and efficacy are not documented in the official literature.
- Proof of payment.

Under the RTCA, analytical standards and finished product samples are requested after registration, according to each country's legislation.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

Three general rules apply to every document:<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a>, <a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

- Every certificate must be valid when filed. If it shows no expiry, it is treated as valid for two years from its issue date.
- Documents must be originals or legalized copies, in Spanish or with a translation. Foreign legal documents must be apostilled or consularized.
- No corrections are accepted on certificates unless the issuing body backs them.

## Alternate manufacturers

The RTCA generally requires one registration per manufacturing site or country, but it notes that this does not apply in El Salvador because the country uses the figure of alternate manufacturing.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup> The SRS tarifario lists adding an alternate manufacturer as its own procedure.<sup><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

## Recognition of foreign approvals

The SRS law allows the SRS to officially recognize regulatory decisions of agencies that international bodies list as reference agencies, and to recognize registrations under the Central American integration instruments.<sup><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

A 2013 regulation, still published on the SRS website, sets a recognition route for medicines registered by agencies certified at PAHO level IV and by the authorities of the United States, Canada, Australia, Switzerland, Japan and the European Medicines Agency. The full Article 20 dossier is still required, and the authority has 10 business days for the technical review of a complete file.<sup><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup> Ask us whether your product qualifies.

## Fees

The SRS tarifario posted on its website lists these prices for obtaining a registration, renewing it or adding an alternate manufacturer:<sup><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

| Product type | Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| Biologics, biotechnological products, vaccines and innovators | 1,500 |
| Generics with bioequivalence | 1,000 |
| Other pharmaceutical products | 600 |

Payments to the SRS are valid for twelve months to complete the procedure.<sup><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup> Confirm the current version with the SRS before paying.

## Validity and renewal

The registration lasts five years.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a>, <a href="#source-3">3</a></sup> Under the RTCA, renewal can be requested at any time before expiry without losing the registration number. After expiry, the product must be filed as a new registration. Within six months after expiry, the holder can ask to keep the original number with a justified cause, but cannot market the product during that period.<sup><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

The Ley de Medicamentos also says an authorization lapses if the holder does not effectively market the product within five years, or if the product leaves the market for three consecutive years.<sup><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
