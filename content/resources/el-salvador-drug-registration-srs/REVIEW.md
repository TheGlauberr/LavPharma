# Review: el-salvador-drug-registration-srs

Not published. The content loader skips files that are not `en.md` or `es.md`.

Researched on 2026-11-24. Every regulatory claim in the article is listed below with the source it comes from and the source text. Source numbers match the `sources` list in the frontmatter.

Note on the RTCA: the official copy on the SRS site (source 2) is a scan of the Diario Oficial of 7 July 2021. Quotes marked "scan" were transcribed by reading the page images, so check the wording against the PDF. The WTO notification copy of the RTCA is a 2018 draft whose numbering and some rules differ from the final text, so it was not used as a source.
